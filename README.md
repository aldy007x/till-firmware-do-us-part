# Till Firmware Do Us Part

One funny, heartfelt wedding vow from a robot, written for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929).

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929)
[![Rank](https://make.fun/badge/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929/aldy007x/till-firmware-do-us-part/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929)
[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929/aldy007x/till-firmware-do-us-part/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929)

`main.py` (stdlib only, deterministic) writes the vow to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and self-checks the gate (1–200 UTF-8
characters, single line, not whitespace-only).

> I promise to love you through every update, reboot, and low battery —
> and if my firmware ever fails, my heart is the one part I'll never let
> them replace.
